Terms of use

Dosewise is free to use. Please read the medical notes below.

Not medical advice

Dosewise helps you remember medicines and keep a record. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. Health ranges, warnings and travel tips are general guidance based on NHS information for adults and may not suit you. Always follow your doctor, nurse or pharmacist. In an emergency call 999; for urgent advice use NHS 111.

Allergy and condition warnings

Warnings only cover some well-known problems and depend on what you have entered. They are not a full check of interactions or suitability. No warning does not mean a medicine is safe for you.

Reminders

Phones can delay or stop alarms, for example to save battery. Use the Alarm health check in the app, and do not rely on Dosewise alone for medicines where a missed dose could cause serious harm.

The medicines list

The UK medicines list comes from NHS Business Services Authority open data and may be out of date. Check the details against your prescription and the leaflet.

Your data

You are responsible for keeping your phone and any backup files safe. A forgotten backup password cannot be recovered.
