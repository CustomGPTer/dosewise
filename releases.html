Release notes

Install a new version over the old one. Your medicines and history are kept.

1.0.2 22 November 2026

  • If Dosewise’s notifications are switched off, an alarm can ring with nothing on screen. Today now shows a red warning when this happens, and one tap switches notifications back on.
  • Dosewise asks for notifications during setup and when you first open Today, and the same warning covers the full-screen alarm and exact-time settings.
  • Opening Dosewise while an alarm is ringing now goes straight to the alarm screen, so you can take, snooze or skip at once.

Download 1.0.2

1.0.1 22 November 2026

  • Dosewise no longer asks for permission to send texts, so Android installs it without the “Harmful app blocked” warning some phones showed for 1.0.0.
  • Backup person: when a reminder goes unanswered, Dosewise now shows an alert with the text ready. One tap opens your messaging app with it written, and you press Send.

Download 1.0.1

1.0.0 22 November 2026

The first public version.

  • Alarms that ring like an alarm clock, repeat and get firmer, with snooze, skip and take from the alarm, notification, widgets or a watch.
  • Every kind of schedule: set times, every few hours, weekly or monthly, cycles, tapering doses, reminder windows and as-needed limits.
  • About 20,000 UK medicines built in, browse by condition, and scanning of boxes and pharmacy labels on the phone.
  • Progress: streaks, badges, a 26-week calendar, patterns and plain-English notes. Full history with corrections.
  • Supplies: stock, refills and repeat prescriptions, with a daily check. Travel mode with home or local time and a packing list.
  • Health: twelve kinds of reading with NHS ranges and charts, a symptom and side-effect diary, appointments, care team and allergies with warnings.
  • PDF report for your GP, emergency card, spreadsheet export and encrypted backup.
  • Smart snooze, spoken reminders, a backup person, six colour themes, family and pet profiles.
  • Privacy: no internet permission, encrypted record, PIN or fingerprint lock, discreet notifications, disguised icon and name, emergency wipe PIN.
