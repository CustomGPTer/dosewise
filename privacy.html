Privacy policy

Dosewise is built so that your health information never leaves your phone unless you choose to send it.

What the app keeps

Your medicines, doses, readings, diary, appointments, contacts, allergies and settings are kept on your phone only. The record is encrypted with a key held by the phone's secure hardware, and it is excluded from Android cloud backups.

No internet

The app does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The developer never receives any of your information.

When something does leave the phone

  • Files you share or save: reports, the emergency card, spreadsheets and backups only go where you send them. Backups are encrypted with your password.
  • Backup person texts: if you switch this on, an ordinary SMS goes to the person you chose, from your phone, through your mobile network.
  • Links: NHS 111, NHS medicine pages and the Yellow Card scheme open in your phone's browser, outside the app.

Camera and photos

Label scanning reads the picture on the phone using a text-reading model built into the app. The photo is deleted after reading and is never sent anywhere.

Permissions

Notifications and alarms (to remind you), starting after a restart, vibration, the fingerprint sensor (if you use it), and sending SMS (only if you turn on the backup person).
