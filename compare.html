Dosewise compared

Medisafe is one of the best-known medicine reminders. These are the differences that matter most if you live in the UK.

DosewiseMedisafe
Cost in the UKFree, everything includedA paid subscription is needed to use the app outside the US, from 1 January 2026: $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year1
Where your record is keptOnly on your phone, encrypted. The app has no internet permissionIn a Medisafe account, which keeps family members' pillboxes in step online2
UK medicines listAbout 20,000 NHS medicines, supplements and UK brands, built into the app and searchable offlineNot described for UK use in its store listing
Emergency wipe and disguiseEmergency wipe PIN, disguised icon and nameNot listed among its features2

Checked 22 November 2026. Prices are as listed by Medisafe in US dollars.
